Heads I Win, Tails You Lose: book site campaign

A test-and-learn lead campaign built around the book site move. The lead offer is confirmed; timing and experiments are still proposed for discussion.

The dev team is moving the book site (headsortailsiwin.com) to our new web platform. Marketing proposes using the move as a proof of concept: a dedicated campaign where we can be experimental, test lead tactics quickly, and carry what works into our other sites and campaigns.

Confirmed The site migration is underway with the dev team.   Confirmed The lead offer is a free physical copy of the book (reader pays shipping only) or the free audiobook.   Proposed Timing, budget, experiments and targets below are still for discussion.

Why this campaign

A safe place to test

A focused site with one clear topic lets us try new offers and pages without risk to our main site.

A fresh start for tracking

The new build is a chance to set up clean tracking and HubSpot capture from day one.

Lessons we can reuse

Winning tactics can roll into blog conversion, webinars and the main site move.

Why now: re-establishing Patrick as an author

Patrick is writing a new book, and it sounds like someone may be coming in November to help him with it. Pushing Heads I Win now re-establishes him as an author with our audience and builds an author framework and audience base we can fall back on when the new book launches. Title and timing for the new book are not set.

Reader journey

Book page visit→Offer: free book (shipping only) or free audiobook Confirmed→HubSpot capture→Email nurture→Webinar, podcast or WealthScore→Conversation with a wealth strategist

Experiments we would like to try

Quiz and WealthScore tie-in Experimental

A short quiz drawn from the book's ideas that leads readers into WealthScore.

A/B landing page tests Experimental

Test headlines, offers and form length against each other to learn what converts.

Retargeting Proposed

Reach book page visitors again with the offer, with clear exclusions and frequency limits, if budget is approved.

Email nurture series Proposed

A short sequence after someone claims the free book or audiobook, expanding on the book's themes and inviting a next step.

Podcast and webinar tie-ins Ready to go

Patrick already mentioned the book in the recent episode Write Your Own Rules, and a book graphic was added to that episode, so he is comfortable promoting it. Podcast and webinar features are a ready channel, not something that needs his approval. A book-themed webinar topic can also be offered in polling.

Tracking, UTMs and HubSpot capture Proposed

Standard campaign naming and UTMs on every link, with forms and conversion events flowing into HubSpot reporting.

Open questions

  • Launch date for the migrated site (from the dev team)
  • Final URL and page structure for the campaign
  • Budget for retargeting and any paid testing
  • Campaign owner and who follows up on new leads
  • How book leads are routed and labeled in HubSpot

How we would measure it

  • Visits by source, tracked with UTMs
  • Offer conversion rate (free book and audiobook claims)
  • A/B test results by variant
  • Email nurture engagement and next steps taken
  • WealthScore completions from book traffic
  • Qualified opportunities and purchases traced to the book site

Baselines and targets to be set once the site is live.
