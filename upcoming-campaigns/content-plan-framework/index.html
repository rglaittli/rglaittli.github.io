Organic-to-paid loopPost organically→Reuse the same post in retargeting→Rotate: each person sees fresh pieces→Strategist feedback→New pieces

Per the SOP: no copyrighted music on organic posts, or they can't be reused in paid. Emails in the window use the same four categories, and email and social content are repurposed into each other.

Short vertical video first

The SOP's core format: short (under 90 seconds) vertical videos for Reels, posted to Instagram and cross-posted to Facebook.

Changes section 7 and 8: video leads; other derivatives support it.

Hook in the first seconds

Open strong. If one piece gets high frequency, the SOP reads that as a weak hook: change the hook.

Changes section 4: every piece gets a tested hook.

Volume and a library

The SOP calls for a large library of short pieces plus a long-form library, so people rotate through fresh content instead of repeats.

Changes section 9: build a library over time, not one-off posts. No volume target set for us.

Long form for depth

Long form adds "safety": podcasts, trainings and the best webinar sections, trimmed for value density (strong open, no filler, an insight every ~45 seconds).

Changes section 8: episodes and webinars get trimmed long-form cuts.

Framing pieces carry no CTA

In the retargeting set, "it's just content meant to help with framing." It sells the next step, not the whole offer.

Changes section 5 for this content type only (see conflicts).

Iterate from feedback

Sales team feedback is the leading signal. Retire weak pieces, refresh from new questions and objections, A/B test.

Changes section 13: add sales feedback to review checkpoints.

Where the SOP differs from this template

Section 5The template gives every piece a call to action (the Wealth Score). The SOP's framing pieces carry none. Both can hold if framing content is treated as its own type.
Section 7The template routes paid through Avalaunch. The SOP's loop reuses our organic posts in retargeting. Who would run it is an open question, and it needs leadership approval.
Section 8The template's derivatives include audiograms, carousels, quote cards and text posts. The SOP is built on short vertical video plus long-form video.
Section 9The template plans by phase (prep, launch, sustain). The SOP's content runs always-on, triggered by each person's action.
Section 11The SOP's high email frequency and some objection tactics (price reframes, financing) need compliance and leadership review before we consider them.
