Proposed tactics

All items below are proposed and not yet resourced.

Episode launch posts Proposed

A consistent post on each social channel when an episode goes live.

Newsletter and email blurbs Proposed

A short episode feature in the newsletter and relevant HubSpot emails.

Clips and audiograms Proposed

Short video and audio clips of the strongest moments for social.

Guest cross-promotion Proposed

Share-ready assets for guests, and appearances on outside podcasts as PR relationships develop.

Show notes and blog posts Proposed

Show notes and a companion blog post per episode, with a CTA matched to the topic.

YouTube and Spotify optimization Proposed

Titles, descriptions, chapters, thumbnails and playlists tuned for discovery.

Content repurposing Proposed

Feed episodes into the internal content program: carousels, articles, Sacred Cows pieces.

Tracking Proposed

UTMs and source tracking so podcast-driven visits and opportunities show up in HubSpot.
