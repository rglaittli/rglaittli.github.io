Write Your Own Rules

A free two-page companion worksheet for the upcoming podcast episode Write Your Own Rules, turned into a small campaign. It's the first free resource like this since Rachel joined, and a low-lift test of how free resources perform.

Pending leadership approval Raised at the October 19 Level 10 Leadership meeting.   Drafted The worksheet draft is in Google Drive (view-only within paradigmlife.net).   Not released The episode isn't out yet, and its final title isn't locked.

Where it comes from

In the episode, Patrick has a moment about writing your rules down. The worksheet gives listeners a simple way to do that, built around two forces every household lives with: certainty and uncertainty.

What's in the worksheet

Step 1 · Notice the rules you already live by

Finish two sentences for your financial life, personal life, and career or business: “In order for me to feel certain, ___ has to happen” and “For me to feel like wow, life is amazing, ___ has to happen.”

Step 2 · Rewrite them into healthier rules

Ask two questions of each rule: is this rule mine, or was it written for me? Does it protect me without keeping me stuck?

Step 3 · Let AI do the interview

A copy-paste AI prompt that walks the reader through the exercise as an interview.

Next step · Get your Wealth Score

The call to action sends readers to portal.paradigmlife.net/wealthscore.

Eight draft posts built only on the worksheet and episode. Every hook lands in the first line or first three seconds. No return or performance claims. Draft copy, not final.
