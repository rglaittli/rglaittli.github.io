Write Your Own Rules

Two forces pull on you for life: the need for certainty and the need for uncertainty. You already have rules for both. Most of them are just unwritten. This free two-page guide helps you see them, rewrite them, and use them to make better decisions.

What's inside

Step 1 · Notice your rules

Finish two sentences for your financial life, personal life, and career or business: “In order for me to feel certain, ___ has to happen” and “For me to feel like wow, life is amazing, ___ has to happen.”

Step 2 · Rewrite them

Ask of each rule: Is this rule mine, or was it written for me? Does it protect me without keeping me stuck? Does it let me say yes to more of what I want?

Step 3 · Let AI do the interview

A copy-and-paste prompt for your favorite AI assistant that asks one question at a time and helps you write your rules down.
