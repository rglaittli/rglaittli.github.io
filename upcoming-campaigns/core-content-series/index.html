Core Content Series (working title)

Patrick is close to finishing new core content that frames everything Paradigm Life does, something that doesn't exist today. Marketing's job is to get it in front of the right people and connect it to the Wealth Score and Family Office 360.

In progress Patrick is finalizing scripts. An AI-produced faceless version comes first so Rachel can get a feel for it, then Patrick records the on-camera VSL and webinars, which he said would be October 20.   Name not final   Source: Patrick's October 19 voice message to Rachel.

The formats

Video sales letter (VSL)

About 10 minutes.

Progressive webinar series

Three parts, each about 20 minutes.

Written piece with audio

An article for readers with an audio voiceover: an audio-book-style piece framing the whole approach.

Where it lives

The Education Center, plus YouTube, with possible paid advertising pointing to it.

Three purposes

1 · Bridge ads to the Wealth Score

Fill the gap between our advertising and someone taking the Wealth Score assessment and getting their report.

2 · Client referral workflow

The dev team adds a simple button to the Family Office 360 portal. It opens a pop-up inviting clients to introduce friends, family and colleagues to Family Office 360 and the Perpetual Wealth Strategy (copy to be written). The client enters a name, phone and email; the referral gets an email inviting them to register for the portal, then receives this series.

3 · A shareable referral asset

Something prospects, friends and family, colleagues, employees and strategic partners can pass along to introduce people.

Referral flow

Client clicks referral button in FO360→Pop-up: name, phone, email→Referral gets portal invitation email→Registers for the portal→Receives the content series
