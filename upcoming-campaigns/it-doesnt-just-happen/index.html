It Doesn’t Just Happen

A short run of social posts that make the case without saying it outright: we're not a policy, we're the people who show up. Not specific client stories; situations like these.

Client consent and compliance: stories must be truly anonymous and used in an abstract way. We describe situations like these, not identifiable client accounts. Compliance sign-off is required before any post is scheduled. Some stories from the source call are intentionally left out of this campaign and must never be used. No post has been produced or scheduled yet.

The idea

1

Most people don't have anyone

Most people move through life's biggest moments without anyone in their corner who has thought it through with them.

2

It doesn't just happen

Relationships where clients include us in their family's life are built deliberately, by a team that shows up at every phase, not just at the sale.

3

Voice

Captions use the idea in our own words. No attributed quotes from team members unless they agree first.
