Social campaign · Proposed · Client consent and compliance

It Doesn’t Just Happen

A short run of social posts that make the case without saying it outright: we're not a policy, we're the people who show up. The positioning is not to tell specific client stories, but to say that we experience situations like these.

Client consent and compliance: stories must be truly anonymous and used in an abstract way. We describe situations like these, not identifiable client accounts. Compliance sign-off is required before any post is scheduled. Some stories from the source call are intentionally left out of this campaign and must never be used. No post has been produced or scheduled yet.

The idea

Most people don't have anyone

Most people move through life's biggest moments without anyone in their corner who has thought it through with them.

It doesn't just happen

Relationships where clients include us in their family's life are built deliberately, by a team that shows up at every phase, not just at the sale.

Voice

Captions use the idea in our own words. No attributed quotes from team members unless they agree first.

The four posts

Suggested order, spaced across one to two weeks so each gets its own moment.

1 · Before She Had a Name on Paper

A new father calls from the hospital the day after his daughter is born, ready to start a policy before she has a Social Security number.
Format: quote card. Strongest opener.

2 · The Countdown

A client funding his daughter's wedding with policy loans sends the team unprompted countdown texts: “50 days!”
Format: countdown graphic or short video.

3 · One Policy Became Four

Met single in his twenties with one small policy; then one for each parent, his wife, and now his newborn.
Format: four-slide carousel.

4 · He Remembered, a Month Later

A client emails a month later to check on a team member and her new baby. It goes both ways.
Format: text-forward quote card. Closer.

Grow the story bank Proposed

A quick poll or prompt to the field and team asking people to share stories like these privately with Rachel. She keeps them private and makes sure each one is vague and anonymized before anything is used. The aim is to grow the campaign from 4 posts to 8–10.

How we would measure it Proposed

  • Engagement and reach by post and channel
  • Profile and site visits from the posts
  • Inbound conversations attributed to the series
