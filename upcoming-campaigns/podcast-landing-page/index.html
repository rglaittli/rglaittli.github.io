What you will hear

This is not a tips show. It is a weekly look at how money actually works in a household, and what changes when you treat it as one connected system.

One theme, explored deeply

Every episode anchors on a single idea and stays there long enough to change how you think about it.

Framework, not opinion

Conversations sit inside the Perpetual Wealth Strategy: cash flow, protection, and asset allocation working together.

Patterns from real households

Drawn from two decades of work with thousands of families, including what quietly fails and what people rarely ask until it is late.

Your host

Patrick Donohoe

Host · CEO, Paradigm Life

Patrick co-founded Paradigm Life in 2007 and built the Perpetual Wealth Strategy from patterns that held up across thousands of households. On the show he thinks out loud, names what is at stake, and holds both sides of a financial tension before landing on what the evidence supports.
