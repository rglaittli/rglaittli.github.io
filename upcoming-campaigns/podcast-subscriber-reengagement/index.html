Email re-engagement and retargeting · Proposed

Old Podcast Subscriber Re-engagement

Re-engage email subscribers from the old podcast list and bring them back to The Perpetual Wealth Podcast, our new content and the Wealth Score.

Owner John (copywriter) owns the email campaign and will take a first stab at the sequence; Rachel reviews.   Email sequence: John drafting

Audience and objective

Audience

Email subscribers from the old podcast list. List size, age and source are TBD.

Objective

Re-engagement: opens and clicks, return listening, Write Your Own Rules guide downloads and Wealth Score starts.

Sequence outline Proposed

A starting point for John, not final copy. Subject lines and order are his call.

Email 1 · The show is back Proposed

“We've been busy.” Reintroduce The Perpetual Wealth Podcast and what's new since they last heard from us.

Email 2 · Value Proposed

Lead with something useful: a top episode or the free Write Your Own Rules guide, once approved.

Email 3 · Invite Proposed

Invite a next step: the Wealth Score at portal.paradigmlife.net/wealthscore, or an upcoming webinar.

Email 4 · Last chance or preferences (optional) Proposed

Ask whether they want to keep hearing from us, and let them choose what they receive.

List hygiene Proposed

Sunset unengaged

Stop mailing addresses that don't open or click after the sequence.

Re-permission if the list is old

If consent is unclear or dated, ask subscribers to opt back in before regular sends.

Unsubscribe and CAN-SPAM

Clear unsubscribe in every email, a valid sender identity and physical address.

Compliance review

Review the sequence before anything sends.

Paid retargeting (optional) Proposed

Upload the list as a custom audience on Meta and YouTube to reach subscribers who don't open email, handled with Avalaunch. Only if consent and platform rules allow it.

Tracking Proposed

UTMs on every link, with clicks, return visits, guide downloads and Wealth Score starts captured in HubSpot under a single campaign name.

Open questions

  • Where the old list lives today
  • How old the list is, and its size
  • Whether we have consent to email it
  • Sender name: the show, Patrick, or Paradigm Life
  • Whether paid retargeting is in scope with Avalaunch

How we would measure it Proposed

  • Opens and clicks by email
  • Return listening (episode plays from email links)
  • Write Your Own Rules guide downloads
  • Wealth Score starts
  • Unsubscribes and re-permission rate

Baselines set once the list is reviewed.
