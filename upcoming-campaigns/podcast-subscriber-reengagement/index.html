Audience and objective

Audience

About 17,000 subscribers from the old podcast list (confirmed), held in Mailchimp. Last engagement was around 2017. Sender name: The Perpetual Wealth Podcast.

Objective

Announce the relaunch: where to find The Perpetual Wealth Podcast now, and that it's back, bigger and better. Then point them to the Wealth Score, where the podcast now sends listeners.

Sequence outline Proposed

A starting point for John, not final copy. Subject lines, order and length are his call. Every email's main CTA is the Wealth Score at portal.paradigmlife.net/wealthscore.

Email 1

The show is back

“We've been busy.” Where to find the podcast now, and what's new. CTA: get your Wealth Score.

Email 2

Top episode

“Whose Dream Are You Living?” with Patrick and Wade Reed (released September 29, 2026): defining what you actually want and clearing “the money fog.” CTA: Wealth Score.

Email 3

Invite

A direct invitation to take the Wealth Score and see where you stand. An upcoming webinar can be a secondary option.

Email 4

Stay with us

A nurture-style “do you want to keep hearing from us?” email. Light structure for John:

  • Thanks for being part of the earlier audience
  • What you get: new episodes, the Wealth Score, occasional resources and webinar invites
  • How often we'll write (to be decided)
  • One clear “keep me on the list” click
  • Unsubscribe always in the footer (required)

Where the list goes Proposed

We treat this campaign as re-permission. Mailchimp only holds the old list; only contacts who engage move to HubSpot.

Unsubscribe and CAN-SPAM
