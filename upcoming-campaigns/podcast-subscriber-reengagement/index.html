Email re-engagement · Podcast relaunch announcement · Proposed

Old Podcast Subscriber Re-engagement

An announcement to subscribers from the old podcast list: here's where you can find us now. The podcast is back. It's bigger. It's better. The podcast is the value; the Wealth Score is the call to action.

Owner John (copywriter) owns the email sequence and will take a first stab at it; Rachel reviews.   Email sequence: John drafting

Audience and objective

Audience

Email subscribers from the old podcast list, held in Mailchimp. List size and age are TBD.

Objective

Announce the podcast relaunch to old subscribers: where to find The Perpetual Wealth Podcast now, and that it's back, bigger and better. Then point them to the Wealth Score, where the podcast now sends listeners.

Sequence outline Proposed

A starting point for John, not final copy. Subject lines, order and length are his call. Every email's main CTA is the Wealth Score at portal.paradigmlife.net/wealthscore.

Email 1 · The show is back Proposed

The relaunch announcement: “We've been busy.” Here's where to find The Perpetual Wealth Podcast now, and what's new. The podcast is the value; the main CTA is to get your Wealth Score, since that's where the show points listeners today.

Email 2 · Top episode Proposed

Feature a top episode: “Whose Dream Are You Living?” with Patrick and Wade Reed (released September 29, 2026), on defining what you actually want and clearing “the money fog.” Link to the episode, with the Wealth Score as the CTA.

Email 3 · Invite Proposed

A direct invitation to take the Wealth Score and see where you stand. An upcoming webinar can be a secondary option.

Email 4 · Stay with us Proposed

A nurture-style “stay with us” email asking whether they want to keep hearing from us. Light structure for John to shape:

  • Thanks for being part of the show's earlier audience
  • What you'll get if you stay: new podcast episodes, the Wealth Score, and occasional resources and webinar invites
  • How often we'll write (to be decided)
  • One clear “keep me on the list” click
  • Unsubscribe is always in the footer (required)

Where the list goes Proposed

Mailchimp is only holding the old list. HubSpot is where engaged subscribers end up.

Old list in Mailchimp→Re-engagement sequence→Engaged and still subscribed: import into HubSpot as contacts

Stays engaged

Anyone who stays engaged and doesn't unsubscribe is moved into HubSpot as a contact.

Unsubscribes

Removed and not imported into HubSpot.

List hygiene Proposed

Sunset unengaged

Subscribers who don't engage during the sequence aren't carried forward.

Re-permission if the list is old

If consent is unclear or dated, the “stay with us” email doubles as the opt-back-in.

Unsubscribe and CAN-SPAM

Unsubscribe in every email footer, a valid sender identity and physical address.

Compliance review

Review the sequence before anything sends.

Tracking Proposed

UTMs on every link, with clicks, episode plays and Wealth Score starts tracked under one campaign name. Imported contacts carry the campaign as their HubSpot source.

Open questions

  • How old the Mailchimp list is, and its size
  • Whether we have consent to email it, or need re-permission first
  • What counts as “engaged” for the HubSpot import (for example, opened, clicked, or clicked “keep me on the list”)
  • When the import happens: after Email 4, or on a rolling basis
  • HubSpot lifecycle stage, source and list for imported contacts
  • Sender name: the show, Patrick, or Paradigm Life
  • Send cadence going forward for those who stay

How we would measure it Proposed

  • Opens and clicks by email
  • Return listening (episode plays from email links)
  • Wealth Score starts
  • Engaged subscribers imported into HubSpot
  • Unsubscribes

Baselines set once the list is reviewed.
