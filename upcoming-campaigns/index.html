Ongoing workstreams

Consistent branding and clean HubSpot data support every campaign. Content feeds campaigns, webinars and the launch; conversion paths and follow-up turn engagement into measurable opportunities.

Blog conversion In progress

Blog traffic is substantial, with no conversions reported. Verify completed actions are captured, then match each CTA to the reader's topic rather than sending everyone to a meeting. Proposed Grok Bot (Rachel's AI assistant) can go back through old posts to add better CTAs and update UTM parameters, with Rachel reviewing the edits.

Existing client re-engagement

One eligible segment and an educational offer, run with John Stewart's pilot team: segmented email, a resource page, short video, FAQs and a team view of interested clients. Paid retargeting only where permissions and audience size allow.

Webinars

Topic polling across six topics, with November sessions being planned. Registration, invitations, presentation, replay and follow-up built as one package.

PR and partnerships

Relevant audiences, appearances and partner promotion, each with a clear, trackable next step; outside podcast guests as relationships develop.

Upcoming events

A rolling list of conferences and speaking opportunities, evaluated for fit, cost, timing and capacity. No commitments yet.

Content production

Internal content alongside Avalaunch's expected 23 pieces per week (not yet reached), ramping toward a proposed 23 per day once counting rules are defined.

Wealth Score attribution

Since the October 13 portal release, Wealth Score contacts come straight from the portal, not a HubSpot form. Reports need to count portal-created contacts, and UTMs plus primary lead source and description should carry through to Salesforce.

How we will measure progress

Source-level reporting, with existing-client expansion separate from new prospects and Avalaunch reported on its own. Baselines and numeric targets come once audience sizes, budget and purchase economics are known.
