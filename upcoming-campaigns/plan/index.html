How I'm executing

Every campaign moves through the same five steps. Patrick signs off on FO360 launches; for everything else he approves the concept and I sign off on launch, so he isn't pulled into every small approval.

1

Draft

I draft the creative and copy, with John on long-form copy. Every visual is proof of concept until it is approved.

2

Jeremy AI review

Each piece is checked against Jeremy AI, built on Jeremy Haynes' Hammer Them method: does it answer a question, follow-up, objection or expectation, with a hook up front? Feedback so far has been positive.

3

Concept approval (Patrick)

Patrick approves the concept for every campaign. Compliance review comes first where a campaign needs it.

4

Launch sign-off

FO360: Patrick signs off the launch. Everything else: I sign off once the concept is approved, including paid ads. UTMs and HubSpot capture from day one.

5

Measure

Track the measures each campaign page names. Baselines first, then targets. A monthly readout (Target) keeps leadership current.

Why this order

  • Family Office 360 first. It is the top priority, so the waitlist package goes to Patrick first for concept approval and launch sign-off, and list building starts as soon as he signs off.
  • Write Your Own Rules goes next week. The episode was held for this campaign: a different episode posts this week, and Write Your Own Rules posts the week of Oct 26, with concept approval and teasers ahead of it.
  • Podcast marketing starts next week too. It begins with the week of Oct 26 episode and runs on every episode after.
  • The Core Content Series VSL lines up with FO360 go-live. The site already names the VSL as the FO360 launch asset.
  • Compliance-heavy work starts review early. The podcast list re-engagement sends its first small batch the week of Nov 16 after compliance, then skips Thanksgiving week. It Doesn't Just Happen launches after Thanksgiving week.
  • Heads I Win launches with the migrated book site, Target Oct 30. The dev team said end of October, but it isn't guaranteed; if the site slips, launch moves with it.
