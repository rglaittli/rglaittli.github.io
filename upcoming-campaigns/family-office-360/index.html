Family Office 360: product launch

Family Office 360 is the rebranded client portal, introduced by leadership at the October 5 to 8 Alley Rallies. The public launch date is not yet set. This page lays out what a complete launch must cover; specifics fill in as the platform details settle.

Success is habit, not sign-ups. Leadership wants households returning to the platform and moving through the four steps each year. Only features live at release are promoted (for example, the Document Vault is an initial storage feature).

Launch phases

A phased launch fits how leadership is rolling out the team model, which is piloted with Team John this quarter. Dates for each phase are not set.

1 · Internal launch

Every staff member can explain FO360 and uses the name consistently.

2 · Soft launch to engaged clients

Pilot-team households and active clients get early access; collect feedback and stories.

3 · Team re-engagement

Household teams reach unrepresented and opted-out households one-to-one as teams come online.

4 · Public launch

Brand announcement, PR, paid media and acquisition push to prospects.

5 · Sustain

Feature releases, education content and annual-step reminders keep households coming back.

Flags to raise early

Opted-out households

They can't receive marketing email, so the launch needs a team-delivered track, not just a brand announcement.

The ad funnel lands on the portal

Avalaunch ads run to /4321, then WealthScore on the portal. A rebrand touches ad copy, HubSpot forms and possibly the URL; confirm any domain change with Mark first to protect attribution.

“Family office” positioning
