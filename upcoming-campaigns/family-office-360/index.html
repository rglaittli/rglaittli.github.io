Honesty rules for FO360 copy

Copy says plainly that FO360 isn't open yet.
No launch date in public copy.
“Founding” or “early access” only if true.
No “limited spots” unless spots really are limited.
Don't promote features that won't be live at release.

Launch phases

A phased launch fits how leadership is rolling out the team model, piloted with Team John this quarter. Internal dates are in Status and timing; no public dates are set.

1

Internal launch

Every staff member can explain FO360 and uses the name consistently.

Proposed Every staff member, marketing included, gets portal access first so they can see it.

2

Soft launch to engaged clients

Pilot-team households and active clients get early access; collect feedback and stories.

3

Team re-engagement

Household teams reach unrepresented and opted-out households one-to-one as teams come online.

4

Public launch

Brand announcement, PR, paid media and acquisition push to prospects.

5

Sustain

Feature releases, education content and annual-step reminders keep households coming back.

What we're borrowing from Multiplier

Garrett Gunderson's Multiplier Family Office (launched July 2024) makes nearly the same promise as Family Office 360: “one point person, one coordinated team, and one clear plan,” for business owners earning $350K+. It's currently his main paid focus. Source: Dan Torsak's competitive teardown.
